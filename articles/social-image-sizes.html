Instagram, YouTube & TikTok sizes explained

Image · ConvertFlow

Platforms crop and scale aggressively. Exporting at the right size avoids blurry edges and cut-off faces. ConvertFlow includes these presets in the Image tab.

Tips

  • Keep important content in the center — Stories and Reels hide edges under UI.
  • For thumbnails, high contrast and large text work better than fine detail.
  • Export JPG for photos or WebP for smaller files with similar quality.
  • Use “Keep aspect ratio” or Contain + black fill so logos are not stretched.

Default fill color in ConvertFlow is black so letterboxed images look natural on dark UIs.
